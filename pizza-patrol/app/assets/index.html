How to Play

You are an animatronic mascot on the night shift at Chompy's Pizza Palace. Some kids are being NAUGHTY (red caps): spilling soda, scribbling on walls and throwing pizza!

  • Move: drag your thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen.
  • SPRINT: hold to run fast. It drains your battery bar.
  • ROAR: freezes naughty kids near you. Careful: it also scares the GOOD kids (party hats), and that costs points!
  • Catch a naughty kid by touching them. They get sent to the Time-Out corner.
  • Catch enough naughty kids before 6 AM, and don't let the MESS meter fill up!

Special tricks:

  • POWER: every head has a special move: Gator CHOMP DASH, Penguin ICE SLIDE, Raccoon PIZZA BAIT, Moose STAMPEDE, Owl NIGHT SIGHT.
  • Vents: step on a metal vent to pop out somewhere else. Sneaky kids use them too!
  • Pizza delivery: naughty kids rush to free pizza (easy catches!). Grab it yourself to recharge everything.
  • The Night Guard (night 3+): when his flashlight is on you, FREEZE. He thinks you're just a costume... unless you move!
  • Tickets: earn them every night and spend them on hats and shades in the builder.

Mix & match parts: Legs set speed, Arms set grab reach, the Head sets how far you see and how big your roar is, and the Body sets your battery.
